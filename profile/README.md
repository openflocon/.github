# 👋 Welcome to Flocon

**Flocon is a powerful desktop companion for Android and Kotlin Multiplatform apps.**
Inspect, debug, mock and control your app in real time, from a single desktop tool.

## What is Flocon?

Flocon connects to your running app and gives you a live view of what's happening inside it, so you can spend less time guessing and more time fixing. It works with Android and KMP targets (desktop and iOS).

## What you can inspect

- **Network**: OkHttp, GraphQL, WebSockets and gRPC traffic
- **Databases**: browse and inspect your app's local data
- **Analytics**: follow the events your app sends
- **Mocking & control**: simulate responses and drive your app's behavior in real time
